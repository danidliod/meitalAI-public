1. Introduction

At "Growing From Within" (meitalAI), we are committed to protecting your privacy and respecting your rights. This privacy policy explains in detail how we collect, use, store, and protect your personal information in accordance with the European Data Protection Regulations (GDPR), the Israeli Privacy Protection Law 5741-1981, and the regulations enacted thereunder.

By using our platform, you agree to the collection and use of your information as described in this policy. If you do not agree to this policy, please do not use the platform.

About us: Growing From Within (meitalAI) is a digital platform that provides personalized guidance services to people at professional crossroads. We operate under Israeli law and are committed to complying with all relevant data protection regulations.

2. What Information We Collect

We collect the following information to provide you with a personalized service:

2.1. Basic Identity Information

  • • Full name: First and last name (as provided during registration)
  • • Email address: Used for identification, login, and communication (via Supabase Auth)
  • • Registration date: For account management and support

2.2. Diagnosis Information

  • • Diagnosis questionnaire answers: All answers you provided in the diagnosis questionnaire (25-30 questions), including text responses, multiple choice selections, and scale ratings
  • • Diagnosis results: Scores across different dimensions (such as: burnout, fears, motivation, attitude toward change), dominant categories, and automated analyses
  • • Personal report: A report generated based on your diagnosis, including insights and recommendations

2.3. Journey Information

  • • Daily reflections: All text you wrote as part of reflections in the 7-day journey
  • • Action outputs: Files you uploaded (such as images, documents), comments on actions, and any other content you shared as part of tasks
  • • Progress tracking: Which days were completed, which actions were performed, and overall progress status
  • • AI feedback: Automated analyses of your reflections and outputs, including insights and recommendations generated by AI systems

2.4. Professional Information (Optional)

  • • Resume: If you uploaded a resume file (PDF, DOC, DOCX), we store the file and process it using AI to extract relevant professional information
  • • LinkedIn link: If you provided a link to your LinkedIn profile, we store the link (not the content of the profile, only the link)
  • • Professional background: Information extracted from resumes or LinkedIn (such as: previous roles, areas of expertise, years of experience) - used to tailor responses and content

2.5. Conversations with Meital-Bot

  • • User messages: All text you sent in conversations with Meital-bot
  • • Bot responses: All responses you received from Meital-bot
  • • Conversation history: All conversations are saved to enable continuity and context between conversations
  • • Conversation summaries: Automated summaries of previous conversations created to provide context to the bot

2.6. Technical Information and Logs

  • • IP address: Stored for security, identifying suspicious activity, and technical support
  • • Browser information: Browser type, version, operating system (for technical support and issue identification)
  • • Technical logs: Records of technical operations, errors, and system activity (without personal content)
  • • Cookies: Only technical cookies for maintaining login session and preferences (not tracking cookies)

Important note:

We do not collect sensitive information such as ID numbers, credit card details, or detailed medical information. All information we collect is relevant only to the service we provide.

3. Why We Collect Information (Processing Purposes)

We use your information only for the following purposes, in accordance with the legal basis for processing personal information:

3.1. Contract Performance and Service Delivery

  • • Creating personalized diagnosis: Processing your questionnaire answers to create an accurate and personalized diagnosis
  • • Creating personal report: Using diagnosis information to create a detailed report with insights and recommendations

3.2. Service Improvement and Enhancement

  • • Personalization: Using your information to tailor content, questions, and responses personally (only within the platform)

  • • Algorithm improvement: Using anonymous information (without personal identification) to improve our algorithms and models

3.3. Technical Support and Security

  • • Account identification and management: Using identity information to enable login, identification, and account management
  • • Security: Using technical information (such as IP) to identify suspicious activity, prevent fraud, and protect your account
  • • Technical support: Using logs and technical information to resolve issues, improve performance, and provide support

3.4. Legal Obligations

  • • Record keeping: Maintaining records as required by Israeli law and GDPR regulations
  • • Legal compliance: Complying with legal obligations, including providing information to authorities if required by law

⚠️ What we do not do with your information:

  • • Do not sell or transfer information to external parties
  • • Do not use information for advertising or marketing
  • • Do not share information with advertising companies or data distributors
  • • Do not use tracking cookies or technologies to track you outside the platform
  • • Do not use information for purposes unrelated to the service we provide

Your information remains within our platform only and is used only for the purposes listed above.

4. How We Store Information

We use secure and professional services to store your information, ensuring maximum security:

4.1. Data Storage

  • • Database: PostgreSQL via Supabase (secure cloud service). All information is encrypted at rest and in transit
  • • Backups: Automatic daily backups are kept for a limited period for recovery in case of failure
  • • Location: Information is stored on Supabase servers (secure regions, usually in Europe or the US, according to Supabase's selection)

4.2. File Storage

  • • Supabase Storage: Files you uploaded (such as resumes) are stored in Supabase Storage
  • • Private Bucket: Files are stored in a private bucket - only you (through your account) can access them
  • • Access restrictions: Even we cannot access your files directly - only through a secure permissions system

4.3. Security Measures

  • • Encryption: All information is encrypted at rest and in transit (TLS/SSL)
  • • Limited access: Only authorized employees with required permissions can access information, and only for technical support
  • • Authentication: All access to information requires strong authentication (via Supabase Auth)
  • • Security updates: We regularly update the system to address new threats
  • • Monitoring: We regularly monitor the system to identify suspicious activity

4.4. Data Retention Period

Your information is stored as long as you use the platform. If you choose to delete your account, we will delete your information within 30 days (except information we are required to retain by law).

Exceptions: In certain cases, we may retain limited information for a longer period if required by law or if there is suspicion of illegal activity.

Note: Although we take all reasonable measures to protect your information, no system is perfect. If you identify suspicious activity or a security issue, please contact us immediately.

6. Your Rights (GDPR and Privacy Protection Law)

In accordance with GDPR regulations and the Israeli Privacy Protection Law 5741-1981, you have the following rights:

6.1. Right of Access

You can request to receive a copy of all personal information we have about you, including:

  • • All information you provided (diagnosis answers, reflections, conversations)
  • • Information generated based on your information (reports, analyses, insights)
  • • Information about how your information is used

Response time: We will respond to your request within 30 days (or earlier if possible).

6.2. Right to Rectification

You can request to update or correct inaccurate, incomplete, or outdated information. You can update some information directly through your account, or request us to update it.

6.3. Right to Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten")

You can request to delete your personal information in the following situations:

  • • The information is no longer necessary for the purposes for which it was collected
  • • You withdraw your consent to process the information
  • • The information was processed unlawfully

Exceptions: We may retain certain information if required by law or if there is a legitimate interest in retaining it (such as fraud prevention).

6.4. Right to Restriction

You can request to restrict the use of your information in the following situations:

  • • You dispute the accuracy of the information (until we verify)
  • • The processing is unlawful and you prefer restriction over deletion
  • • We no longer need the information, but you need it for legal claims

6.5. Right to Object

You can object to the processing of your information if the processing is based on our legitimate interest or a public purpose. If you object, we will examine the request and stop processing unless we have compelling reasons to continue.

6.6. Right to Data Portability

You can request to export your personal information in a structured and readable format (such as JSON or CSV), so you can transfer it to another service or save it locally.

6.7. Right to Withdraw Consent

If the processing of your information is based on your consent, you can withdraw consent at any time. Withdrawal of consent will not affect the lawfulness of processing done before withdrawal.

6.8. Right to File a Complaint

If you believe we have violated your rights, you can file a complaint with the privacy protection authority:

  • • In Israel: Privacy Protection Authority - gov.il/privacy
  • • In the European Union: The data protection authority in your country

How to exercise your rights:

To exercise your rights, contact us at: privacy@meitalai.com

We need to identify you to ensure we provide information to the right person. We will respond to your request within 30 days (or earlier if possible).
